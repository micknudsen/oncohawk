# Oncohawk

Domain language for Oncohawk analysis and its reusable inputs.

## Language

**Oncohawk workflow**:
The analysis workflow for tumor-only short-read DNA whole-genome sequencing of blood cancers, using sequencing inputs and prepared bundles to produce a report for expert review.
_Avoid_: Report-generation workflow, which understates the analysis included in this workflow.

**Reference bundle**:
A reusable collection of reference sequences and associated assets for analysis. Its contents are limited to the needs of the current milestone.

**Knowledge bundle**:
A prepared collection of biological and reporting knowledge associated with a reference bundle.
