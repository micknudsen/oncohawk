## Final acceptance checkpoint

Completion of #28 and then #25 requires the following observed evidence against the final source, retained in the linked PR and issue delivery record:

- Exact #28 commit/PR and merge SHA, with a passing CI link for the final reviewed head.
- Completed full public acceptance/configuration suite count and duration, plus pinned-module integrity result.
- Exact output-tree and real diagnostic-content checks for default/custom/relative roots, diagnostics disabled, and both analysis selections.
- Collision rejection with unchanged preexisting sentinels, and completed-sample BAM/index usability after another sample fails with diagnostics enabled.
- Reference configuration/output/provenance isolation and retained scientific/input-error coverage.
- Independent Standards and Spec review results, sequential child delivery, and combined parent acceptance decision.

Required CI remains the delivery gate. SLURM/Apptainer execution and full-human-reference measurements are informal operator checks and were not performed for these recorded slices. Knowledge compilation, variants, annotation, sample reports, new diagnostic generators, and clinical validation remain outside this migration.
